# Training & Placement Policy 2026-27

## 1. Registration
Every student must register on the T&P portal before the start of the placement season. Students who do not register cannot apply to any campus drive.

## 2. One Student One Job
Once a student accepts an offer, they are considered placed. A placed student may apply only to a "dream offer", defined as an offer whose CTC is at least 1.5 times the CTC of the offer already held. A student can avail at most one dream offer.

## 3. Backlogs
Students with active backlogs are eligible only for companies that explicitly allow them in their eligibility criteria. Backlog status is verified by the examination cell on the day of the drive.

## 4. Attendance and Conduct
Registering for a drive and then failing to attend the test or interview without prior intimation leads to debarment from the next two drives. Use of unfair means in any online test leads to debarment from the entire placement season.

## 5. Offer Acceptance
Students must accept or decline an offer within 48 hours of the result announcement. Declining an offer after acceptance is treated as misconduct.

## 6. Internships
PPO (pre-placement offer) holders are treated as placed from the date the PPO is accepted.
